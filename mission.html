What we believe

Mission

Craft, dignity, and a real path to a career in food.

Louma is a bakery built around the people who make it. They train on the line, work in plain view of every customer, and get paid like the professionals they're becoming. Many of our teammates have intellectual or developmental differences — and the bakery exists, in part, to prove what that looks like done right.

  1. 01

    Craft over corners

    European technique, Levantine flavor, every shift. The work is hard on purpose — and it should be.

  2. 02

    Dignity at the counter

    Above LA living wage, full-time hours, and a name on every name tag. Everyone here is a professional, training or otherwise.

  3. 03

    Community on the corner

    A real neighborhood place — the kind you walk to, that knows your order, that closes when we sell out.

100%

On-the-job training

Every teammate trains on the line, paid from the first shift — no unpaid trial periods.

Real wages

Above LA living wage

We pay above the LA living-wage floor. Tips top it up — they don't make it up.

Full-time hours

40 a week, year-round

Hours that add up to a paycheck — and a career that gets harder, and more interesting, over time.

Each teammate trains alongside our head bakers and baristas. They learn lamination, espresso, customer service, and how to run a station from open to close.

We hire to keep. Full-time hours, real wages, benefits, and a job that gets harder and more interesting over time.
