Careers

Come bake with us — full-time hours, real wages, a job that gets harder and more interesting over time.

Craft, every shift

Lamination, espresso, sourdough — you learn from people who've trained in Paris, Beirut, and the best kitchens in LA.

Dignity, on payday

Above LA living wage, paid from your first shift. Tips top it up — they don't make it up.

Hours that add up

40 a week, year-round. Predictable schedules. A real paycheck that builds into a real career.

A team that shows up

Small crew, no hierarchy theatre. Everyone here takes pride in what comes out of the oven and looks out for each other.
